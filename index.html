Introducing the new AIQ brand

Energy that thinks

 

Team,

Ahead of a public reveal in the coming weeks, we want to walk you through AIQ’s brand refresh and why it’s happening now.

AI brands have spent the last few years competing on capability: power, performance, technical superiority. That’s no longer enough. As adoption matures, capability is becoming the bare minimum, not a differentiator. Every serious player has it.

What sets the next generation of leading brands apart is different: trust, empathy, human relevance — how well they connect, not just how well they perform.

Six years in, with real operating experience across domains and markets, AIQ needs a new platform built for that shift.

What’s changing

Brand vision

The era of intelligence: energy with empathy, powering responsible progress

Brand purpose

When energy thinks, humanity advances

Tagline

Energy that thinks

The new visual identity — warmer tones, the energy that powers everything, the impact it has on the world — reflects the same shift: from purely technical to more human. The goal is a brand that feels as intuitive and approachable as the technology itself.

Public rollout begins in the coming weeks, with the full unveiling at ADIPEC 2026, where AIQ again co-hosts the AI Zone.
